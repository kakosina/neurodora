# Code of Conduct

Neurodora should be a place where people can discuss unusual creative technology seriously and safely.

## Expected behavior

- Be specific, respectful, and constructive.
- Critique work and decisions, not a person's identity or competence.
- Make room for different experience levels and communication styles.
- Respect privacy, consent, authorship, and media rights.
- Disclose relevant conflicts of interest.
- Accept a maintainer decision without harassment; disagreement may be documented calmly.

## Unacceptable behavior

- harassment, threats, stalking, discrimination, or sexualized attention;
- publishing private information or credentials;
- impersonation, deceptive endorsement claims, or deliberate attribution removal;
- repeated disruption, spam, or bad-faith legal/security threats;
- submitting media or code that you know you have no right to contribute.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, reject contributions, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban a participant when necessary to protect the project and community.

Report conduct concerns privately through [Telegram](https://t.me/neurodora). Include links or screenshots and avoid spreading sensitive material more widely than necessary.

Enforcement decisions should consider context, impact, prior behavior, willingness to repair harm, and the safety of affected people.
