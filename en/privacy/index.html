PRIVACY POLICY

This page explains what data may be processed when you visit neurodora.com and why.

Last updated: September 23, 2026

At a glance: the site has no registration, user accounts, subscription form, or payments. If you submit an inquiry to the atelier, you provide your name, contact details for a reply, and a description of your request.

1. Who is responsible for the site

The neurodora.com website brings together the independent AI project Neurodora and the atelier. For privacy inquiries, you can contact the project via Telegram.

2. Visitor analytics

The site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to view overall page traffic and technical loading metrics.

According to Cloudflare, this service does not use cookies or localStorage for analytics, does not fingerprint visitors, and does not collect or use their personal data. For more information, see Cloudflare’s official documentation.

3. Technical processing

Hosting, content delivery, and site security providers may process technical information needed to deliver pages and maintain security, such as IP addresses, request times, browser types, and requested URLs. This processing is performed by the respective providers under their own policies.

4. Atelier inquiries

The form sends the name, contact details, and project description you enter to a Cloudflare Workers handler. After verification, the message is delivered to the atelier’s owner through Telegram so they can respond to your inquiry. If you choose the email option, a prepared message opens in your email app for you to send yourself.

Cloudflare Turnstile is used to protect the form from automated spam. The verification token and technical request data, including the IP address, are checked. For more information, see Turnstile’s data processing information. For questions about data submitted in an inquiry, you can email neurodora@gmail.com.

5. External links

The site contains links to Telegram, YouTube, and other external platforms. Once you visit them, those services’ privacy policies and settings apply.

6. Policy changes

This policy may be updated if new features or services are added to the site. The current version is always published on this page with its last updated date.
