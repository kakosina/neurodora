Why cute rock is pop music’s final form

Pop wanted the perfect hook

The entire history of popular song is a hunt for a phrase you can’t forget.

Cute rock gets that technology ready-made and isn’t shy about using it.

All music was heading toward cute rock. It just spent a very long time pretending it had other plans.

Rock wanted physical energy

Rock proved you can feel music in your body. Volume, attack, drums, guitar — all of it turns a song into an event.

Cute rock takes that too.

The final form of pop music is suspiciously logical.

The internet wanted closeness

Social media taught people to get used to knowing far too much about other people’s lives. We know who ate what, who they didn’t reply to, which photo they deleted.

Cute rock turns that everyday closeness into song material.

And the puzzle falls into place

Hooks from pop. Physical energy from rock. Angst from emo. Closeness from the internet. Permission to be stupid from meme culture.

If someone with a poor sense of moderation wrote music history, this is exactly how they’d end it.

Pop wanted hooks. Rock wanted noise. The internet wanted closeness. Cute rock took it all.
