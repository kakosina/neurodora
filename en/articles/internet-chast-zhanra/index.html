The internet is part of the genre

Messages, stories, deleted chats, and voice notes aren’t trendy props. For a personal song today, the internet is just a normal place where relationships happen.

importantDon’t list apps.

If the lyrics just name five platforms, they’ll age faster than the interface.

When most relationships happen through a screen, pretending that real lyrics have to take place on a bench in the rain is pretty strange.

The internet is everyday life now

“Was online,” “deleted a message,” “listened to a voice note,” “saved a photo” — these are actions just like “didn’t turn up” or “forgot the keys.” Their power isn’t in being technological. They actually happen.

The internet suits cute rock especially well: it’s full of tiny events. A reaction without a reply. A story view. A message typed and never sent.

A digital detail needs to be specific

catalogue
“You’re online, in chats, in stories, in the feed — our digital world.”
action
“You watched my story a minute later. So you did have your phone.”

How to keep a song from aging in a year

You can use app names if they matter. But behavior lasts longer. Interfaces change; the habit of checking whether someone has replied stays.

It helps to mix the digital with the physical: the narrator deletes the chat but still keeps the ticket; changes their profile picture but doesn’t give back the hoodie.

The internet changes the form

Lyrics can be built from short fragments, like a chat. A voice note can become a long, stumbling sentence. A notification can become a short, repeating sound in the arrangement.

The internet is interesting as a place where people act strangely.

the rule

A digital detail works if it changes the scene. If you can replace it with the word “internet” without losing anything, it’s decorative.
